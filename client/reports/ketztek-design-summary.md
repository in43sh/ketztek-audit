# ketztek.com: How the Website Looks and Reads

Checked on Saturday 21 November 2026, from the public website only, on simulated phones and a desktop screen. We did not contact you or log in anywhere.

## What works

Your logo with the yellow double "z" is distinctive, and the calm sand, white and near-black colors suit a careful, practical firm. All text is dark enough to read easily. The site respects visitors who turn off animations and stays usable when zoomed to twice its size. The copy is specific and honest, which is rare.

## The three changes that matter most

**1. Make the phone menu and the booking buttons work.** On phones, the "EN" language link sits on top of the menu button, so a tap opens nothing. At the end of the home page, the "Book a 30-minute call" button has no link. Both are quick fixes, and both stop visitors who are ready to talk to you.

**2. Put your offer and the booking button on the first screen.** A visitor first sees your logo twice and a short slogan; the first booking button appears only after a full screen of scrolling. A short headline saying what you do, with the button right under it, lets a busy manager act at once.

**3. Tidy the inner pages and the contact form.** On narrow phones the contact form is wider than the screen, so the page wobbles sideways, and its Send button looks unstyled. The Expertise page has no side margins, so text touches the screen edge and runs very long lines on a computer. Text also switches between left and right alignment. One consistent page width and left-aligned text fix most of this.

Smaller points: a purple color sits beside your yellow logo, phone text is a little small, one action has different button names, and the photos are stock images rather than your own work.

## What would help

- Photos from site visits, workshops or tools you have delivered, if they may be shown.
- Your logo files and any colors you consider part of your brand.
- A decision on whether the current look should stay; we would keep it, with these fixes.

## How we checked

We measured fonts, colors, spacing and buttons on six pages at several screen widths, tested every button and link on phone screens, checked keyboard use and zoom, and compared three similar firms' home pages. We did not check the booking calendar on a real phone or anything behind a login.
