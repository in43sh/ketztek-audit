# ketztek GmbH: Your Website and Online Presence

Checked on Friday 20 November 2026, from public pages only. We did not contact you, log in anywhere or submit any form.

## 1. The short version

**What works.** Your website is clear and honest. It says plainly what you do, for whom, and that you will tell a client when a job is small. The free 30-minute call with an online calendar is an easy first step. The site loads quickly, runs on current software, and your address and company details match everywhere we looked.

**What costs you enquiries.** A few small faults stop visitors before they reach you. On a phone, the menu button cannot be opened. The address `www.ketztek.ch` shows an error page. The last "Book a 30-minute call" button on the home page does nothing. Beyond those, the site is in English only for buyers who mostly search in German, and nothing on it shows past work, the people behind the firm or what clients say.

**If you only do three things:**

1. Make `www.ketztek.ch` open your website.
2. Fix the phone menu and the last booking button.
3. Complete your Google listing (phone, hours or "by appointment", photos, a short German description).

## 4. What is costing you customers and enquiries

### The menu cannot be opened on phones

On phone-sized screens, the "EN" language link sits on top of the menu button. We tested common phone widths: a tap on the button hits "EN" instead, and the menu stays closed. A visitor on the Contact or Expertise page has no way to reach the other pages, because the footer only links to the privacy page and Impressum.

**Fix:** move the language switch away from the menu button on small screens (or into the menu), and add "Contact" and "Book a call" links to the footer. Small.

### `www.ketztek.ch` shows an error

Your email addresses end in `ketztek.ch`, so people will type `www.ketztek.ch`. That address shows a browser security warning and then a "page not available" error from your hosting provider. We saw the same error three times that morning. Your privacy page also links to it. The address without "www" works and forwards to `ketztek.com`.

**Fix:** in your hosting account, connect `www.ketztek.ch` to the website, or forward it to `ketztek.com`, with its own security certificate. Small.

### The last booking button does nothing

At the end of the home page, under "Not sure where to start?", the "Book a 30-minute call" button has no link. The same button higher up the page works. The reader who has gone through the whole page and is ready to book is the one who gets nothing.

**Fix:** link the button to your booking page and test every button after each change. Small.

### The website is only in English

Every page is written in English. The "DE" switch translates the page automatically in the visitor's browser; it worked in one of our three tries, and search engines never see that German text. When we checked Google's search suggestions in Switzerland, 23 of the 45 German search phrases we tried were suggested exactly as typed, and none of the 6 English ones were. Eight of the nine similar firms we looked at write their sites in German first.

**Fix:** write German versions of your pages yourselves (you already write German on your booking page), give them their own addresses so search engines can find them, and keep English as the second language. Medium.

### Nothing shows past work, people or reviews

A buyer about to hand a core process to an outside firm wants to see that you have done it before. Today the site has no example project, no client or sector result, no team section and no certifications, and the photos are stock images. There are no Google reviews yet. Eight of the nine similar firms point to case studies or references from their home page.

**Fix:** add two or three short project stories (the problem, what you built, hours saved), even without client names; introduce the team by role with real photos; list any certifications; ask your first clients for a Google review. Medium.

### Menu links that only work on the home page

"Expertise" and "Services" in the menu jump to sections of the home page. On every other page they do nothing. Your separate Expertise page is not linked from the menu at all.

**Fix:** point "Expertise" to the Expertise page and "Services" to the services section of the home page. Small.

### Google listing and directories are nearly empty

Your Google listing shows the right name, address and website, but no phone, hours, photos or description; Google even prompts visitors to add them. On local.ch your entry is unclaimed and shows only the address, and search.ch has no entry. Similar firms in the region show phone numbers, hours and photos.

**Fix:** claim and complete the Google listing (a German description, phone if you want calls, hours or "by appointment", office and team photos, your services); claim local.ch; add search.ch. Small.

### No phone number

There is no phone number on the site, Google or the directories. Some buyers, especially in construction, call before they book. Seven of the nine similar firms show a number.

**Fix:** if you want calls, publish one business number in the footer, Impressum and listings. Small.

### Search results show only "ketztek"

The home page's title in search results is just "ketztek", no page has a short description for search results, and link previews in email or chat show no image. A placeholder "Website is under Maintenance" page is also listed for search engines.

**Fix:** write a title and a two-line description for each page (in German and English), add a share image, and remove the placeholder page. Small.

### The booking page is confusing

The site promises "a free 30-minute call", but the booking page offers four choices: "Demo Software" (no description), "Coffee Chat", "Free Initial Consultation" and a one-hour demo. The descriptions also show stray asterisks around "EN" and "DE".

**Fix:** hide "Demo Software", link the site's buttons to the free consultation directly, and remove the asterisks. Small.

### The privacy page still refers to the old website

The privacy page describes `www.ketztek.ch`, mentions Google Analytics (which your site does not use), and does not mention the services that the site does use (spam protection, the translation widget, the booking calendar). Two of its links, labelled with your email address and privacy address, open a login page of your hosting provider's old site builder.

**Fix:** update the page for the current website and the services it uses, with plain email links. Whether you want legal review is your decision; this is not legal advice. Small.

### Slower on phones than it needs to be

Pages respond quickly, but each page downloads an 855 KB font file that is never stored by the browser, and the home page's main content takes about 3.3 seconds to appear in a phone test (Google's "good" mark is 2.5 seconds). The contact page loads about 6 MB because the booking calendar loads straight away.

**Fix:** use a compressed web font, let browsers keep images and fonts, and load the booking calendar only when a visitor asks for it. Small.

### No way to see what the website brings in

No visitor statistics or click counting run on the site, so you cannot tell how many people visit, which page they read or how many click "Book".

**Fix:** add a simple, privacy-friendly statistics tool and count clicks on the booking and contact buttons. Your booking calendar and Google's search tool already hold some numbers. Small.

### Standard security settings

The site does not send a few standard security settings that browsers support, and a default file shows the website software's version. Nothing suggests a problem today; it is routine housekeeping.

**Fix:** add the standard settings in the hosting configuration and hide the default files. Small.

## 6. What we recommend

1. **Fix the listings and addresses first.** `www.ketztek.ch`, the Google listing, local.ch, the booking page and a phone number need no website work at all.
2. **Then fix the current website.** Your website software is current and fast, and most problems above are small edits. A new website would not fix any of them by itself.
3. **Then add German pages and project stories.** This is mostly writing that only you can do. It can be done on the current site.

A rebuild would make sense only if you want a larger, German-first site with many service and project pages and would rather not maintain it yourselves.
